# Dashboard timeout and wake

Without a sensor, choose **Settings → Display & units → Auto-turn off**, tap **Confirm**, and return to the dashboard. Tap anywhere when it goes dark. The first tap only wakes it. **Never** disables the timeout. For hands-free wake, add the optional sensor as follows.

1. Unplug USB power and disconnect the battery, if fitted. Use the four-pin **I2C (H1)** connector. Waveshare specifies a **HY2.0 4P** cable. Match the printed signal names; cable colours and module pin order vary.
2. Connect H1 **3V3 → VIN/VCC** (the breakout's 3.3 V-compatible supply input), **GND → GND**, **SDA → SDA** and **SCL → SCL**. Do not use a 2V8 regulator output as VIN. XSHUT and GPIO1/INT are unused on breakouts with XSHUT pulled high. If your board has different supply markings or lacks that pull-up, check its schematic before connecting. [Waveshare schematic](https://files.waveshare.com/wiki/ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-7B/ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-7B-Schematic.pdf) · [Connector guide](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-7B/FAQ).
3. Aim the exposed sensor window toward the approach path; keep the bezel, glass and nearby furniture out of its view. Restore power. In **Settings → Display & units**, move your hand in front of the sensor. The status line should change to a distance in millimetres. “No target in view” means the sensor is communicating. **Settings → Debug mode** also shows the live distance and saved wake threshold.
4. Under **Auto-turn off**, select **30 sec / 1 min / 2 min / 5 min / Never** and select **Wake distance** (30–200 cm, every 10 cm). Tap **Confirm**. Defaults: **1 min** and **100 cm**. Return to the dashboard. For a quick check, select 30 sec, stand away, let the screen go dark, then walk within the selected wake distance.
5. A tap or a new approach wakes the dark dashboard at reading brightness. During timed viewing it stays at that level until the next idle shutoff, even when the dim schedule is active. With Never, it returns to the scheduled level after one minute without another tap. The timer starts again on a new approach or touch; standing still in front of the display does not keep it awake. Step back at least 15 cm beyond your chosen wake distance (or out of view) for about one second to re-arm it. Only the backlight switches off; time and weather continue updating. The setup/settings screens remain lit, and a missing or disconnected sensor leaves tap-to-wake and the idle timeout active. The firmware retries detection every 10 seconds. If the live distance barely changes, check orientation and wiring; if the wake is too dim, check both brightness sliders.

Above 100 cm the firmware uses the [Pololu long-range profile](https://github.com/pololu/vl53l0x-arduino/blob/1.3.1/examples/Single/Single.ino). The selected distance is a threshold for valid returns; lighting, reflections and the target affect the distance that can actually be measured.
